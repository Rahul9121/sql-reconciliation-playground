Welcome to the SQL Reconciliation Playground

I built this so you can write and run real SQL in the browser against two deliberately messy systems: a trade blotter and a ledger.

There are twelve guided challenges covering anti-joins, full outer joins, duplicates, amount mismatches with a tolerance, sequence gaps, window functions, NULL handling, CTEs, and top-N per group. You can also switch to free-query mode and explore the schema on your own.

Everything runs on your device with DuckDB-WASM. Nothing is uploaded. The data is synthetic — not real or employer data.

Pick a challenge, write SQL, hit Run (Ctrl+Enter), then Check my answer. Hints and solutions are there if you want them.
